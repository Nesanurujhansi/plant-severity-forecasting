# Data Card: PlantVillage

## Dataset Identification
- **Dataset Name**: PlantVillage
- **Authors**: Sharada P. Mohanty, David P. Hughes, Marcel Salathé
- **Hugging Face Repository**: `mohanty/PlantVillage`
- **Verification Status**: UNVERIFIED (Date: 2026-09-20)

## Content & Description
- **Content**: 54,303 lab-style images of healthy and diseased plant leaves across 14 crop species and 26 diseases under controlled lab lighting conditions.
- **Configurations**: Color, grayscale, segmented.

## Project Policy & Role
- **POLICY**: Lab-style images are strictly limited to initial vision backbone pretraining and basic unit test stubs. They are **NEVER** used for headline accuracy claims or in-the-wild severity evaluation.
