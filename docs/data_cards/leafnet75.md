# Data Card: LeafNet75 Segmentation Repositories

## Dataset Identification
- **Dataset Name**: LeafNet75 Mask Repositories
- **Hugging Face Repositories**:
  1. `LeafNet75/Leaf-Disease-Segmentation-masks` (Binary lesion masks, Apache-2.0, ~54 MB)
  2. `LeafNet75/In_the_Wild_masks600` (SAM 2 automated leaf masks, MIT, <1,000 images)
- **Verification Status**: UNVERIFIED (Date: 2026-09-20)

## Content & Role
- **Content**: In-the-wild leaf photos accompanied by SAM 2 (Segment Anything Model 2) auto-generated binary leaf masks and manual lesion masks.
- **Project Role**: Auxiliary training data for Module 1 leaf mask and lesion mask segmentation heads.
